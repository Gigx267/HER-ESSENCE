From Order To Doorstep

How Delivery Works

Six simple steps from the moment you message us to the moment your package is in your hands.

1

You Message Us

Tell us what you need on WhatsApp at 07011951017. Ask questions, get guidance, or just send your order list. There's no rush and no pressure to buy.

2

We Confirm & Total

We confirm availability, send you the total (products + delivery fee), and answer any last questions. You get your Order ID (e.g. HE-2026-1042) at this stage.

3

We Pack Discreetly

Your items are sealed in a plain, unmarked package with no brand name or product description on the outside. Tamper-evident seal for your safety.

4

We Dispatch

Orders confirmed before 2pm on weekdays are dispatched same day. You'll get a WhatsApp message with your tracking status and rider details (for Abuja orders).

5

We Deliver

The rider calls or messages you before arriving. If you prefer, we can drop at a pick-up point, office reception, or with a trusted neighbour — whatever feels safest for you.

6

We Follow Up

Two days after delivery, we check in to make sure everything's fine and answer any usage questions. This aftercare is free, always.
